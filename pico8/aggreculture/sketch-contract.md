# Aggreculture sketch contract

```text
Sketch name: aggreculture-homestead-v1
Primary question: Does reclaiming seeded wilderness into persistent farmland make exploration serve the farming loop?
Premise: A farmer grows a morning harvest while hungry blights invade the field.
Verbs: move, face, travel, cycle tool, open tool belt, reclaim terrain, till, plant, harvest, swing hoe
Room shape: numeric seed selector followed by a connected 3x3 screen overworld with the homestead at its center
Start: choose a world seed, generate the run, then arrive beside the farmhouse
Goal: expand onto useful terrain and harvest the generated crop quota
Failure/reset: eaten crops lose progress; the win screen offers an immediate restart
Out of scope: larger/infinite maps, procedural quests, NPCs, inventory screen, seasons, economy, saves, bosses, permadeath
```
